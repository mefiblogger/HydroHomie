# Commercial licensing

HydroHomie is released under the [GNU General Public License v3.0 or later](LICENSE).
That licence is free to use, and for most people it is the only one that matters:
fork it, modify it, learn from it, ship it — go ahead.

The GPL has one condition. If you distribute HydroHomie, or anything derived from it,
you must release your **complete corresponding source** under the GPL as well, and
grant your users the same freedoms you received.

If you want to build on HydroHomie **without** publishing your source — in a closed
commercial product, or under App Store terms that conflict with the GPL — you need a
separate commercial licence. That licence is available. It is not free.

## Who needs one

You probably need a commercial licence if you intend to:

- ship a closed-source app that reuses HydroHomie's code
- distribute a derivative through the Apple App Store as a proprietary product
- bundle it into a product whose licence terms are incompatible with the GPL

You do **not** need one to fork the project, contribute to it, use it privately,
or publish your own GPL-licensed derivative.

## Why the App Store matters here

Apple's App Store terms impose usage restrictions that are widely held to conflict
with the GPL — VLC was removed from the store over this in 2011. In practice this
means a GPL-licensed fork of HydroHomie cannot be shipped on the App Store. A
commercial licence resolves that.

This restriction does not apply to the copyright holder, who may licence the work on
any terms.

## Why this is possible

Dual licensing only works while one party holds copyright over the entire codebase.
That is why contributions require a licence grant — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
